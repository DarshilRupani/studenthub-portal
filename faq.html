COMMON QUESTIONS

How can we help?

Answers to some frequently asked questions.

01

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a digital campus companion that helps students access important academic information and campus activities in one place.

02

How do I create a StudentHub account?

Open the Register page from the navigation menu, enter your student information and submit the registration form.

03

What can I see on the dashboard?

The dashboard provides quick access to student notices, timetable information and study materials.

04

Where can I find upcoming campus events?

Visit the Events page to view upcoming technical activities, competitions and other campus events.

05

Can I update my profile information?

Yes. Open the Profile page and use the Update Information section to edit your basic student details.

06

What should I do if I have a problem?

If you cannot find an answer here, visit the Feedback page and send your question or feedback to the StudentHub team.
