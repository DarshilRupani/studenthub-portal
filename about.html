About StudentHub

StudentHub is a web portal built to help college students manage academic and campus life digitally. It brings notices, timetables, study materials, and student profiles together in one place.

Our Mission

To reduce dependency on scattered notice boards and messaging groups by giving students and faculty a single, organized platform.
